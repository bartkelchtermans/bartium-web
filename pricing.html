Frequently asked questions

What do you do with my personal data?

Bartium handles your services exceptionally well, but it only does that. Other services or your Bartium client cannot and will never see what happens within a service. Learn more

Will I need Enterprise?

If you are using Bartium for personal use in a non-commercial environment, most likely not. Enterprise is meant for any business of any size.

What if I want an Enterprise feature in my Basic plan?

You would probably be best off with a custom plan, contact us and we'll get you up and running in no time.

How do I get started with my Enterprise plan?

We like to have a chat to set up your Bartium clients according to your needs and make sure everything is up to standard. Start here

Could you implement 'this' feature?

Bartium is in active development and we're always open to hear what cool features you would like to see. Contact us and we'll see what we can do.
